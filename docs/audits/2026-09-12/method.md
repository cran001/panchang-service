# Audit protocol, fixed before new comparisons

Audit date: 2026-09-12. Subject: current dirty working tree, production defaults VSOP87 + Meeus lunar series, Lahiri, Gregorian civil dates, Java IANA zones, sea-level unobstructed horizon unless explicitly stated. No expected value is derived from the subject.

Existing gates retain their original bands. New USNO comparisons use 30 seconds from the published minute as a rounding-compatibility test, not a bound on true error. Report signed residuals and minimum residual outside the rounding interval. Meteorological/terrain differences are not measured. New Horizons longitude screening: solar 1 arcsecond, lunar 12 arcseconds (existing claimed measured envelope); this is an empirical screening threshold, not a guaranteed envelope. Latitude and distance residuals are descriptive, with no invented accuracy threshold. UTC-versus-UT1 and updated Earth-orientation predictions remain part of the time-argument comparison.

New observance comparisons require exact fasting dates. For compatibility with existing fixtures, solar timing bands remain -15 to +75 seconds and lunar bands -180 to +75 seconds; those accommodate a known reference discrepancy and are NOT astronomical accuracy tolerances. Also report every unadjusted difference. Published minute truncation is the repository hypothesis, not independently proved for every generator. Start and end statistics are separate, using absolute residual to the printed value, conventional median and nearest-rank p95. No filter removes large residuals. Raw obsolete-DST times are retained; a separate identified subset excludes them from the main timing distribution. Missing windows, missing caps and extra actual windows are explicit rows.

Bounded new evidence plan: 2027/2028 Horizons January-to-February daily samples; ten-minute geocentric samples bracketing Vrindavan's closing full moon and Auckland's Dvadashi end; USNO held-out Cape Town, Singapore and Reykjavik at year boundaries/solstices plus London/New York DST dates and the two disputed sites. Attempt same-lineage 2027/2028 Mayapur and Hyderabad calendars, using cached bytes first. Unavailable responses stay in the evidence manifest. No substitution of another publisher to obtain agreement.

Reference fields without compatible definitions are reported as unavailable or convention-unresolved. Daily reference tithi and nakshatra labels are compared descriptively; the source's unspecified sidereal convention is not silently treated as approved Lahiri. No religious ruling follows from numerical proximity.
